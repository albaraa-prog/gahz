# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

DevQuiz Studio exists to help people learn. We want everyone to be able to take part in this project
without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and explain your reasoning.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully and assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work, and contribute only content you have the right to share.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers through the contact details on the
repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this Code of
Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful.
