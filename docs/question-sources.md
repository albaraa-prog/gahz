# Where the interview questions come from

Gahz questions are written by contributors in their own words. We do not scrape sites or copy question text.
This page explains why, and records the research behind the interview-focused banks.

## Why not scrape?

- **Terms of use.** LinkedIn, Glassdoor and most Q&A sites forbid automated collection, and much of their
  content is behind a login.
- **Copyright.** A question's wording belongs to whoever wrote it. Gahz is MIT licensed, so everything in
  the repo must be free for anyone to reuse. Text copied from a blog, a course or a paid question bank is not.
- **Quality.** Lists on the web are mostly open questions with no answer options. A Gahz question needs one
  correct answer, three plausible mistakes and an explanation, in English and Arabic.

Topics and ideas are not copyrightable. So we research **which topics really get asked**, then write new
scenario-based questions about them.

## Research for the interview banks (November 2026)

The banks `oop-design-patterns`, `system-design`, `frontend-html-css-react`, `testing-code-review-agile` and
`behavioral-interview` cover themes that appeared on three or more of these public lists:

- Wiz Academy, "30 common software engineer interview questions for 2026"
- DataCamp, "Top software engineer interview questions"
- Coursera, "Software engineer interview questions"
- Indeed Career Guide, "Software engineer interview questions"
- Codecademy, "Software engineer interview questions"
- freeCodeCamp, "How to prepare for React technical interviews"
- roadmap.sh, "Top JavaScript interview questions"
- Mimo, "Front-end developer interview questions"
- Back-End Developer Interview Questions (GitHub, community list)
- Front End Interview Handbook (GitHub, MIT)

Recurring themes: OOP and design patterns, system design and scalability, caching, databases and distributed
systems, closures and async JavaScript, the DOM and React hooks, web security, testing and code review, SDLC
and Agile, and behavioral questions (teamwork, conflict, failure, explaining to non-technical people,
prioritization, learning new technology). Themes Gahz already covered (data structures, SQL, REST, networking,
JavaScript) were not repeated.

No text from these pages was copied. If you ever adapt material from an openly licensed source, check that the
license allows it, keep the required attribution, and list the source and license here.
